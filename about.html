About Me

I am a backend engineer with a focus on building robust, scalable systems. My journey started with Python and Django, falling in love with its "batteries included" philosophy. Recently, I've been exploring the elegant world of Laravel, finding joy in its expressive syntax and powerful ecosystem.

Philosophy

I believe that good software is boring. It works predictabily, fails gracefully, and is easy to understand. I prioritize:

  • Readability over cleverness: Code is read more often than it is written.
  • Reliability over features: A feature that breaks the system is a bug.
  • Simplicity over complexity: The best code is the code you don't write.

Experience

Over the years, I've worked on high-traffic APIs, data processing pipelines, and complex business logic implementations. I'm comfortable diving into database optimization, setting up CI/CD pipelines, or debugging obscure race conditions.

Personal

When I'm not coding, I'm likely spending time with my family, reading about history, or trying to brew the perfect cup of coffee. I value a balanced life where technology serves us, not the other way around.
